Blue Hills at the northwest edge of Saratoga

Blue Hills is a residential neighborhood in northwestern Saratoga. Wikipedia places it there, and the name is shared by Blue Hills Elementary School. A real estate portal says it borders Cupertino and Highway 85, with homes backing toward the foothills.

The same portal reports ranch and two-story homes, a median build year around 1970 and a median lot of about 10,900 square feet. Treat those as a portal's estimates. A neighborhood of that vintage is a good reason to ask about the water heater and the water pressure, which are the two easy checks below.

Two checks that matter in a house of this vintage

Water pressure

The California Plumbing Code calls for a pressure-reducing valve where static pressure can exceed 80 psi. A gauge on a hose bib takes a minute to read and shows what your fixtures see.

Water heater age

Many tank heaters last around a decade, sometimes longer. If yours is older, check for rust at the base and damp insulation. See water heater replacement before it fails.

Drain history

Slow drains that return after cleaning point to a deeper problem. A camera run can show whether it is the house line or the lateral.

Which agencies serve a northwest address

Blue Hills borders Cupertino, so the agency that handles sewer service for your address may differ from neighbors across town. According to the Santa Clara County LAFCO service review, West Valley Sanitation District serves about two thirds of Saratoga and Cupertino Sanitary District serves the rest. We cannot tell you which applies to your street, and the district can.

For a backup, shut off water use, find the cleanout and call a plumber to run a camera. If sewage appears in more than one home, the public main may be the issue and the district should be called. See drain cleaning for clears and sewer camera inspection for the camera.

Parks and landmarks that help with directions

Kevin Moran Park and Azule Walking Park sit near Highway 85, and Blue Hills Elementary is a familiar landmark. Give the plumber your street and ZIP 95070 and mention if the driveway is steep or gated.

For the rest of the city, read the Saratoga plumbing home page and the service areas page. For leaks you cannot trace, leak detection explains the process.

What to have ready when you call

A few details make the first call faster and the advice more accurate. Know the street address, the fuel type of the water heater and roughly how old it is. Note where the main water valve sits and whether it turns. If water is visible, a photo helps.

Say what changed. A new noise, a smell, a spot on the ceiling or a bill that jumped all narrow the search. Mention gates, steep driveways and pets so access is simple. Ask how a visit is priced before you agree to it, and ask what the plumber will check first.
