Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Saratoga, CA

A blocked main line affects every drain in the house. Our crews clear roots, grease and debris from the lateral and tell you how to keep it from returning.

If every drain in your house is slow, a toilet gurgles when you run the shower or sewage is surfacing at a low drain, the main sewer line is blocked. Our line is open 24/7. Stop using water, keep the dishwasher and washing machine off and call. The blockage will not clear itself, and each flush adds more to the backup.

Sewer line cleaning is different from sink-level drain work. The lateral runs from your foundation to the public sewer connection, usually four inches wide and fifty to a hundred feet long or more on hillside lots. It needs larger machines, and it needs a plan for what comes after the blockage is gone.

Why main lines clog

Roots

Fine roots find the joints in clay and older pipe and grow into the flow. Saratoga lots carry large trees, and roots are a common cause of lateral blockage on older clay or concrete pipe.

Grease and scale

Fat that cooled and hardened lines the walls over years. The channel narrows until a small clog stops it completely.

Wipes and paper products

Flushable wipes do not break down. They catch on rough spots and root hairs and build into a mass.

Pipe damage

A sag, an offset joint or a crack gives debris a place to settle. Cleaning treats the blockage, a camera shows the cause.

How we clear a main line

Find the cleanout

We open the cleanout near the foundation or at the property line. A clear access point speeds the work and keeps mess out of the house.

Cable the blockage

A heavy drum machine with a cutting head breaks through roots and packed debris and restores flow.

Scour the pipe

For grease and scale we follow with a jetter that washes the walls clean. See hydro jetting.

Camera check

A camera run confirms the line is open and shows whether the pipe needs repair.

While you wait for the crew

Keep the situation from getting worse.

  • Stop using water. That includes the dishwasher, washing machine, showers and toilets.
  • Clear the area around the cleanout so the crew can reach it quickly.
  • If sewage is on a floor, keep people and pets away and open windows if you can do so safely.
  • Do not pour drain cleaners into the pipe. They do not clear roots and add danger for the crew.

Preventing the next blockage

Keep grease and wipes out of the drain. Run cold water for a few seconds after using the garbage disposal. Do not plant fast-growing trees or shrubs over the lateral. Roots seek water, and a sewer line with open joints is the best source around.

If roots have been a problem, a scheduled cleaning every year or two can keep them in check before they cause a backup. A camera check after cleaning shows how fast they are returning. Homes near the Village and Saratoga Creek, where trees are large and pipes are older, often benefit from a regular schedule.
