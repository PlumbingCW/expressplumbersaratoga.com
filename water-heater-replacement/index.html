Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Saratoga, CA

A tank that is leaking from the shell cannot be repaired. We swap gas, electric and tankless units, haul the old one away and tell you the price before the work begins.

  • Same-day swaps when in stock
  • Old unit hauled away
  • Seismic strapping included
  • Clear price before work starts.

If water is pooling around your tank right now, close the cold water valve above the heater, switch the heater to off or pilot, and call. Our line is answered 24/7, and a leaking tank is one of the calls we move to the front of the schedule. A rusted shell that weeps cannot be patched, so the question becomes how fast a replacement can be in your garage or closet.

Most replacements take a few hours when the new unit matches the old one in fuel type and size. Changes like moving from a tank to a tankless unit, relocating the heater or adding a new vent take longer, and we explain that before we start.

Signs the tank is finished

Water at the base

Moisture that returns after you wipe it up, rust streaks or a stained drain pan point to a failed tank liner. Shut off the cold water and call.

Rusty or cloudy hot water

Brown water only from hot taps often means the tank is corroding inside. If cold taps run clear, the heater is the likely source.

Age past ten years

Tanks often start failing between eight and twelve years. Old units can fail without much warning, so planning a swap on your own schedule beats a flooded closet.

Repairs stacking up

A second repair in a year on an older tank usually costs more than the first month of a new unit once you count the lost hot water.

What a replacement visit includes

Shut off and drain

We close the gas or power, shut the water, and drain the old tank safely to a floor drain or the yard.

Remove and haul away

The old unit comes out and leaves with us. You do not need to arrange disposal.

Set and connect

The new heater is set on a pan or stand, connected to water, gas or power, and fitted with a new expansion connection and shutoff if the old ones are worn.

Brace and test

The tank is strapped to meet California seismic rules, the vent and gas joints are tested, and the temperature is set before we leave.

Earthquake bracing is part of every swap

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped to resist falling or horizontal displacement. Saratoga sits close to the San Andreas fault in the Santa Cruz Mountains, so strapping is not a formality here. We install it on every replacement and check it on repair visits.

Permits, venting and local rules

Replacing a water heater in kind generally needs a permit in California cities. Ask the City of Saratoga building division what applies to your address, since requirements for gas vents, drain lines and expansion tanks can change with the project. We pull permits where they are required and schedule any inspection.

Hard water matters too. San Jose Water Company serves most of Saratoga, and its groundwater source is roughly three times harder than Valley Water imported supply. A new tank on a groundwater-fed street benefits from a yearly flush, which keeps sediment from baking onto the bottom of the tank.
