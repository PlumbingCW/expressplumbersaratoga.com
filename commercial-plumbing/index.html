Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Saratoga, CA

A closed restaurant kitchen, a flooded office or a retail floor with sewage on it costs money by the hour. We respond after hours for Saratoga businesses and property managers.

  • 24/7 emergency line
  • After-hours response
  • Restaurants, offices, retail and rentals

When a business has a plumbing failure, the question is not only how to fix it but how fast you can reopen. Our line is answered 24/7, so a backed-up kitchen on a Friday night or a burst line over a holiday weekend reaches a person, not a voicemail. Shut off the water at the main or the affected fixture, move inventory and electronics out of the way and call.

Commercial plumbing differs from residential work in scale and in rules. Pipes are larger, systems are shared among tenants, health codes apply to food and medical spaces and a failure can affect several neighbors in a building. Below is what we handle and what we need to know when you call.

Types of business we serve

Grease lines, floor drains, dishwashers and prep sinks. Kitchens in the Village and along Saratoga-Sunnyvale Road need drains that handle heavy use.

Offices and medical suites

Restrooms, break rooms and water heaters, often shared. Fast response matters because tenants share the cost of downtime.

Retail and shopping centers

Restroom fixtures, floor drains and roof drainage. Long runs of shared sewer lines are common.

Rental and multifamily properties

Water heater failures and stoppages that need quick access coordination with tenants.

What to do before we arrive

A few minutes of preparation saves time on site.

  • Close the main water valve or the valve for the affected area if you know where it is.
  • Clear access to the main shutoff, cleanouts, the water heater room and the affected fixtures.
  • Tell the dispatcher whether people are on site, whether the space has to reopen quickly and if tenants share the line.
  • Photograph the damage and note the time you first saw it. Property managers and insurers will ask.

Planned work for commercial properties

Not every call is an emergency. We handle fixture replacement, water heater swaps, drain cleaning and line repairs on a scheduled basis. For recurring problems, hydro jetting and a camera inspection tell you what is happening in the pipe and whether a regular cleaning schedule would help.

Grease traps and interceptors for food businesses need regular cleaning and can overflow when neglected. We can arrange scheduled cleaning, and if a trap overflows after hours, call the emergency line. Backflow testing is required for many commercial water connections and must be done by a certified tester. Ask us about it when you book, and we will tell you what applies to your property.

Landlords, tenants and shared lines

Who pays depends on the lease and where the problem sits. A clog in a fixture usually belongs to the tenant. A clog in the shared main belongs to the building. A camera run shows where the blockage is, and we document what we found so each side can see it.

Part of Saratoga is served by West Valley Sanitation District, according to the Santa Clara County LAFCO service review. Businesses near the border of service areas should ask the sanitation district which agency covers their connection. For permit questions on commercial work, the City of Saratoga building division is the contact.
