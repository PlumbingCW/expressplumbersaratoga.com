Inside Saratoga

Saratoga covers valley blocks, the Village along Big Basin Way, the Golden Triangle between Saratoga Avenue, Saratoga-Sunnyvale Road and Cox Avenue, and the hillside neighborhoods of Congress Springs and Parker Ranch. The Saratoga Woods and Kentfield areas fall inside our core response area too. We do not give each neighborhood its own page, since the plumbing issues follow the age and slope of the house more than the street name.

If you are not sure your address is covered, call the 24/7 line. The dispatcher will confirm in one question.

Los Gatos

Los Gatos is the neighboring foothill town, reached by Highway 9. Its page covers the 95030, 95032 and 95033 ZIP codes, the older downtown buildings and the hillside lots. Read emergency plumber Los Gatos for the full detail.

What changes from one area to another

Plumbing problems follow the age of the house and the slope of the lot more than the street name. A home near the Village along Big Basin Way is more likely to have older pipe. A home in the hills toward Parker Ranch is more likely to have a long sewer lateral and high water pressure. A home in the Golden Triangle, between Saratoga Avenue, Saratoga-Sunnyvale Road and Cox Avenue, is more likely to have a newer system with problems at the heater or the fixtures.

The dispatcher asks about the age of the house and the type of problem on every call, so the right tools and parts come with us. In Los Gatos, Monte Sereno and Campbell the same questions apply. Part of Saratoga and all of Los Gatos, Campbell and Monte Sereno fall inside West Valley Sanitation District, according to the Santa Clara County LAFCO service review. Sewer questions in those places may involve the district.

ZIP codes we serve

The core ZIP code is 95070 for Saratoga. In Los Gatos we cover 95030, 95032 and 95033. Campbell uses 95008. If you are unsure which ZIP applies to your street, give the dispatcher your address, and they will confirm. Emergency calls from the edge of the area are answered the same as calls in the middle. The dispatcher tells you honestly how soon a crew can arrive.

Landmarks we use to find you

Crews navigate by main roads and landmarks. Highway 9 connects Saratoga to Los Gatos, and Highway 85 crosses the north side of Saratoga. Saratoga-Sunnyvale Road runs through the Golden Triangle, and Big Basin Way runs through the Village. Montalvo Arts Center and Hakone Estate and Gardens sit in the hills above the Village. If you give the dispatcher a nearby landmark along with your address, finding a hillside driveway is quicker.

Getting between towns

Highway 9, the Los Gatos-Saratoga Road, links Saratoga and Los Gatos, and Highway 17 runs through Los Gatos beside Los Gatos Creek. Route 85 crosses Saratoga with a single ramp at Saratoga Avenue, so crews often use Saratoga-Sunnyvale Road and local streets instead. We say this plainly because arrival time depends on the route, the hour and the traffic, and we do not promise a fixed window.

If the call is a true emergency, say so when you call. Tell the dispatcher what is happening and whether water is still running. The crew with the right equipment and parts for that problem is sent, not just the closest truck.

What to tell the dispatcher

Give your street address and ZIP code, since several streets share names across the Saratoga, Los Gatos and Campbell area. Say what you see, hear or smell. Say whether the main valve is closed. Mention hillside driveways, gates and dogs so the crew can get in quickly. For rentals, say who can let the plumber in.

Beyond the core area

Calls come in from Cupertino and Los Altos as well. The dispatcher decides honestly whether a crew can reach you in a reasonable time, and if not, says so rather than promising what we cannot do. A plumber who arrives late with the wrong parts helps no one in an emergency.

For planned work like a water heater installation or a camera inspection, distance matters less, because the visit is scheduled. Call and describe the job, and the dispatcher will book a day that fits.
