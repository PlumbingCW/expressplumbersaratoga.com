Emergency Hydro Jetting in Saratoga, CA

When a cable only punches a hole, high-pressure water finishes the job. We jet drain and sewer lines to strip grease, scale and roots from the pipe wall.

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A cable breaks through a clog and leaves a channel. Hydro jetting uses a hose with a rotating nozzle that sprays water against the pipe wall at high pressure. The water cuts grease, flushes loose scale and shreds soft root growth, then carries all of it downstream.

If your drain keeps clogging weeks after cabling, or your sewer line is backing up because grease has built up in layers over years, jetting is the repair that treats the cause. It is available around the clock for backups, and it can also be scheduled as maintenance for restaurants, rentals and homes with a history of problems.

Call right away if

These signs mean the pipe needs more than a plunger.

  • Multiple fixtures are draining slowly or backing up at the same time.
  • A previous cable cleaning only worked for a few weeks.
  • Water or sewage is rising at a floor drain or in the lowest tub.
  • A camera has shown grease, scale or root mass in the line.

Jetting compared with cabling

Cabling

  • Fast, lower cost for a simple clog.
  • Good for opening a fully blocked line first.
  • Can snag and scratch some pipe if the wrong head is used.
  • Leaves coating on pipe walls.

Hydro jetting

  • Washes the whole pipe interior.
  • Treats grease, scale and soft roots.
  • Needs a known pipe condition first.
  • Often follows a camera check.

When jetting is not the right call

Water at this pressure is not for every pipe. A cracked, collapsed or heavily offset line can be damaged further, or can leak water into the soil. For that reason we usually run a camera before jetting a line we have not seen. If the camera shows a break, we plan sewer line repair instead.

Older cast iron and clay lines may be too fragile for high settings. We adjust pressure and nozzle to the pipe. In some old cast iron lines, mechanical descaling is safer. That is why we ask what the pipe is made of, when it was built and whether it has been cleaned before.

Where grease and roots hit Saratoga homes

Kitchen grease is a quiet problem in houses with long laterals. The fat cools and hardens in the pipe far from the sink, then catches wipes and paper. A hillside lot with a long run to the connection gives grease more distance to settle. Large trees along the lateral give roots more places to enter.

Part of Saratoga is served by West Valley Sanitation District, according to the Santa Clara County LAFCO service review. Jetting your own lateral will not clear a blockage in the public main, but it will tell us whether the problem is yours. If a street-wide backup is suspected, we tell you to call the sewer agency.
