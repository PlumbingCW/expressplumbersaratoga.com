Plumbing help in the Quito area of Saratoga

Quito is a residential part of Saratoga that homeowners tie to Quito Road and El Quito Park. The park has ball fields, picnic areas and the Saratoga Community Garden, and it gives the area its everyday name. Local real estate guides describe single-family homes in Craftsman, ranch and Mediterranean styles, plus some townhouse complexes.

No official map fixes the edges of Quito, so this page uses the road and the park as anchors. One real estate portal reports a median build year near 1951 and a median lot near 9,600 square feet for its Quito Village area. Those are the portal's estimates for its own boundary. They are not a survey of every house, and your home may differ.

A mix of decades under one roof

An Eichler tract of about 35 homes sits off Cox Avenue near Quito Road, on Columbine Court, Shubert Drive and De Havilland Drive. According to a mid-century home specialist, those houses date to 1964 and 1965. Other Quito streets hold ranch homes and older cottages.

Houses of this age have often had kitchens, baths or additions done since they were built. One house can therefore hold pipe and fixtures from several decades. We do not assume what is behind your walls. The first job is learning what you have, then explaining the options.

Sewer service around Quito

Two districts share sewer service in Saratoga. According to the Santa Clara County LAFCO service review, West Valley Sanitation District serves about two thirds of the city and Cupertino Sanitary District serves the rest. Which one serves your street depends on the address, so ask the district before assuming.

West Valley Sanitation District has a project called Quito Basin Q3 and Q4. Its 2020 environmental document describes rehabilitating about 14,000 feet of clay sewer line laid in the 1950s and 1960s in residential parts of Saratoga. The document lists locations only as numerous, so it does not tell you whether your street is included. It also covers public mains, not the pipe inside your house.

If a drain backs up in more than one fixture

Multiple slow drains point to a shared line. These steps limit the damage.

  • Stop running water in the house, including the washer and dishwasher.
  • Look for a cleanout cap near the foundation or in the yard and keep it clear.
  • Ask a plumber to run a camera from the cleanout. See sewer camera inspection for how it works.
  • If sewage surfaces or neighbors have the same problem, call the sewer district for the public main.
