A clogged drain turns into an emergency the moment water has nowhere to go. Our line is open 24/7, so a backed-up kitchen sink at 10 p.m. or a gurgling tub on a Sunday gets answered by a person, not a voicemail box. While you wait for your appointment, stop running water in the house and keep the washing machine and dishwasher off.

Most clogs in Saratoga homes fall into two groups. A fixture clog sits in one sink, tub or toilet and can usually be cleared with a cable. A main line clog sits further downstream and shows up in several fixtures at once. Knowing which one you have decides the tool, the time and the price, so the first questions we ask are about where the water is backing up.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

More than one fixture is affected

A toilet that gurgles when the shower drains, or a tub that fills when the washing machine empties, points to the main drain line. That is a same-day situation, because every flush and every load of laundry adds more water to a line that is already full.

Water is coming up through a floor drain or tub

Sewage backing up inside the house is a health problem as well as a plumbing one. Keep children and pets out of the room, and call right away.

Nothing drains at all

A sink that holds water for hours is fully blocked. A plunger helps with a soft blockage, but if two tries do nothing, stop and call before you push the clog deeper.

The clog keeps coming back

If a drain cleared last month and is slow again, something is catching on the pipe wall. Grease buildup, roots or a bellied section of pipe are the usual causes, and a camera look shows which one.

Do this while you wait for the plumber

Small steps now keep a bad clog from becoming a wet floor.

  • Stop using every drain in the house, including the dishwasher and the washing machine.
  • Skip the chemical drain cleaners. They can sit in a blocked pipe and splash onto skin and eyes when the cable goes in.
  • Find your cleanout, a capped pipe near the foundation or along the side of the house, and keep the area around it clear.
  • Put towels at the base of a toilet or tub that is close to overflowing, and keep children and pets away from standing water.

Where the clog sits decides the method

Drains run from each fixture into a branch line, and branch lines feed the main line that leaves the house and joins the sewer lateral. A clog in a branch affects one fixture. A clog in the main affects everything below it.

Snaking, cabling and hydro jetting: how we choose

A drain snake is a flexible steel cable that spins through the pipe. A small hand-crank version handles most sink and tub clogs. Larger drum machines carry heavier cable with cutting heads for kitchen branches and main lines, and they punch a hole through the blockage so water can move again.

The trade-off is that a cable opens a channel but does not always scrape the pipe wall clean. Grease can coat the sides and narrow the pipe again within months. When a line keeps clogging after cabling, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to wash the walls clear, and a sewer camera inspection shows whether the pipe itself is damaged.

Cable only: what it does well

  • Fast for one-time clogs and soft blockages.
  • Lower cost for a single stubborn fixture.
  • Good first step to restore flow right now.

Add jetting or a camera when

  • The same line clogs again within a few months.
  • A cable keeps snagging at the same distance.
  • Grease or scale coats the pipe wall.
  • Roots show up on camera and need cutting back.

Kitchen sink clogs and the garbage disposal

Kitchen drains are the most common call we get, because cooking grease cools and hardens inside the trap and the branch line. A sink that drains slowly after you run hot water is usually carrying a layer of grease and food solids. If the sink backs up into a second basin or the dishwasher, the clog is past the trap and needs a cable.

If you have a garbage disposal, a clog may sit in the disposal itself or just after it. Our garbage disposal repair service covers jams, leaks at the flange and units that hum without turning. Dishwasher air gaps, which sit on the sink deck on many installations, can also leak when the drain line behind them is blocked.

Shower and tub drains, and leaks in drain lines

Hair and soap scum build up in the first foot or two of a tub or shower drain. A small cable usually clears it. If a tub drains slowly even after the stopper has been cleaned, the clog may sit lower where the tub drain meets the branch line.

Drain lines can also leak. Water stains on a ceiling below a bathroom, a musty smell in a cabinet or a wet spot that appears only after you drain the tub are signs the drain line itself has a crack or a loose joint. A cable will not fix that, so we pair the cleaning with a look at the pipe. For hidden moisture, see our leak detection page.

What Saratoga drain lines are up against

Saratoga sits on the foothills of the Santa Cruz Mountains, so many lots are sloped, wooded and set well back from the street. A long run of lateral pipe between the house and the sewer main gives roots more places to find a joint, and slopes can shift pipe over the decades.

The Village area along Big Basin Way holds some of the oldest buildings in the city, and older drain lines are more likely to have rough interiors that catch grease and paper. In newer parts of town, like the neighborhoods around the Golden Triangle, drain problems more often come from what goes down the drain than from pipe age.

Part of Saratoga is served by the West Valley Sanitation District, according to the Santa Clara County LAFCO service review. If neighbors on your street are seeing backups as well, the clog may be in the public main and the sewer agency should hear about it. We can tell you which side of the property line a clog sits on once the camera has run.
